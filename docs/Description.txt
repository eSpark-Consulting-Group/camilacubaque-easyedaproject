We have a prototype design which we are looking to improve in a new revision in preparation for production in larger quantities.

The PCBA is a small (12.5 x 10.5 mm), 2-layer board. The PCBA has two limit switches, a Bluetooth SoC, and battery.

This project will be comprised of 6 goals:
1. Replace the 2 existing switches with replacements.
2. Incorporate a soft-latching circuit (or similar) to ensure the SoC is connected to battery power when one of the switches is open, and that a long press of the same switch disconnects battery power.
3. Incorporate pads (or similar) to facilitate programming in production.
4. Evaluate battery holder selection.
5. Board review for assembly cost reduction.
6. Generation of manufacturing files and support of prototype production.

We can provide existing BOM and EasyEDA project files.
